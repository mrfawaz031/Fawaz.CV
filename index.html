Project Coordinator in the telecom sector, currently supporting the Salam Mobile project at Mina Excellence. Started in Operations and was promoted to Project Coordinator within just four months based on performance and initiative. I combine hands-on experience in employee data management, sales analysis, and attendance tracking with technical web development skills. I hold a Computer Networks Diploma from King Saud University and an active certification from the Saudi Council of Engineers.
